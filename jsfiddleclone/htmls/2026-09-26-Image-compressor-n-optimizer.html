Per-channel bit depth (R/B coarser than G)
Human eyes are more sensitive to green. Ignored when YCoCg or block-adaptive is on.
YCoCg luma/chroma split
Quantizes luma finer than chroma. Takes priority over per-channel. Ignored when block-adaptive is on.
Block-adaptive bit depth
Per 16×16 block: finer in busy areas, coarser in flat areas. Takes priority over the other depth modes.
Downscale then upscale back
Softens detail (and often compresses better) while keeping the original dimensions.

Multi-path encode, pick smallest
Encodes both a true-color and an indexed candidate (when palette is on) and keeps whichever is smaller.
