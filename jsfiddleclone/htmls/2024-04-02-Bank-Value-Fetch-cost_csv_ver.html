🏦 Bank CSV Parser

Upload a CSV export (semicolon‑separated) with columns: Buchungstag, Name Zahlungsbeteiligter, Verwendungszweck, Betrag, Waehrung. The parser extracts negative Euro amounts, shows details, total, and a categorized pie chart.

📋 How to use: Choose a CSV file (semicolon delimited, UTF‑8) and click “Parse CSV”. The parser reads the header, identifies the relevant columns, and only processes transactions with negative amounts (e.g. -5,21). The description is built from Name Zahlungsbeteiligter and Verwendungszweck for better context.
